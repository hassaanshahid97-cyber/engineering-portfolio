# Aero vehicle performance study, Boeing C-17 Globemaster III

**Nov 2023 to Jan 2024 · aero vehicle performance, Institute of Space Technology · graded 90 of 100**

## Scope

Performance analysis of a heavy military transport across the complete flight envelope:

| Segment | Analysis |
|---|---|
| Steady level flight | Thrust and power required against available, maximum and minimum speed, ceiling |
| Accelerated flight | Energy height and specific excess power |
| Turning flight | Load factor limits, sustained and instantaneous turn rate, corner speed |
| Climbing flight | Rate and angle of climb, time to altitude |
| Take-off | Ground roll, rotation, obstacle clearance distance |
| Landing | Approach, flare, ground roll with reverse thrust |

## Outcome

A complete performance picture built from published geometry, engine data and standard atmosphere
models, with each result traced back to its governing equation and assumption.
