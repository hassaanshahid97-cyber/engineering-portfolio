## Specification

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Configuration | High-wing monoplane |
| Airfoil | NACA 0015 |
| Maximum load factor | 3g |
| MTOW | 2.4 kg |
| STOL take-off distance | 38 m |

## Systems

- **Automation:** Level 1 autonomy on Pixhawk, covering take-off, landing, stabilise and return to launch
- **Mission:** payload-drop mechanism with servo-actuated precision targeting
- **Structure:** carbon fibre composite primary structure, laid up in house

## Why a symmetric section on a transport aircraft

NACA 0015 is an unusual choice for an efficiency-focused airframe, and it is a manufacturing decision
as much as an aerodynamic one: a symmetric, thick section is tolerant of hand layup, gives depth for
the spar, and behaves predictably in the inverted and high-alpha attitudes a student-piloted aircraft
will see. The 3g limit load and the 38 m STOL requirement then set the structure.

## Relevance

Composite design and fabrication, autopilot configuration, mission system integration, and flight test
as the pilot of the aircraft you designed. Directly relevant to TMAL07 Prototype Realization and to
any thesis with a build phase in it.
