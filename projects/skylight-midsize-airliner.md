# SKYLIGHT new midsize aircraft, conceptual design

**Jan 2026 to Jun 2026 · TMAL51 Aircraft Conceptual Design, Linköping University**

## The aircraft

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Passengers | 252 |
| Cruise Mach number | 0.77 |
| Take-off weight | 212,957 kg |
| Certification basis | FAR Part 25 |

## Work performed

- Initial sizing from mission requirements, constraint analysis over thrust to weight and wing loading
- Drag polar build-up, parasite and induced components across the flight envelope
- Weight estimation by component build-up and iteration to a converged take-off weight
- FAR Part 25 flight envelopes: V-n diagram, gust envelope, take-off and landing field performance
- Trade studies on wing geometry and engine sizing against block fuel and field length

## Skills demonstrated

The full conceptual design loop under a certification basis, carried through to a converged and
internally consistent aircraft rather than a single sizing pass.
